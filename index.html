Glide cone simulator

This is the same picture as the glide cone in XCSoar. The white dot with the pink ring is the glider. In Simulator mode it stays where you put it.

Top-left buttons

Glide button — tap to cycle Combined → Single → Off. Long-press opens glide parameters and visualisation.

  • Combined: every airport in view is computed. Click an airport in the cone to turn it off or on again.
  • Single: click an airport to compute that one only.
  • Off: nothing is computed.

Basemap button — tap to cycle map styles. On the altitude gradient style, long-press opens gradient min/max settings.

On a computer

Mouse over shows the worst-case path back.

Click places the glider.

On a phone

Tap shows the worst-case path back.

A long press places the glider.

Drag pans the map.

What the colours mean

  • Pink path: the lowest altitude you would have to be in the air at to be in reach of the airport.
  • Dotted black: you stand by foot on the mountain; from there you would be in reach of the airport in a glider.
  • Coloured path: path of highest arrival at the airport, or at an option. Colour according to context, otherwise green.

  • Option area: every point you can pass through and still reach the airport. Two visualisation modes:

    • Margin: green where you have the most height left above the cone, red where you have none.
    • Degraded masks:
      • Red: reachable only at full L/D
      • Yellow: still reachable at 90% L/D
      • Green: still reachable at 80% L/D
  • Red discs: critical points — ridges or passes you would make at L/D, and that you would not make at 80% L/D.

Live position

Use Flight, Viewer, or Simulator / IGC in the Glide Cones menu. Help stays available in every mode.

  • Flight: GPS is the glider. Navboxes and options follow you. Pointer explores.
  • Viewer: no glider. Pointer explores the cone (minimum alt, elevation, height above cone).
  • Simulator / IGC: place the glider or load a log. Navboxes and options follow the glider. Pointer explores options.
